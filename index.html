Privacy Policy — Mosquito Swat

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Data we collect

Mosquito Swat does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data off your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no behavioral analytics.

2. Camera permission

The game requests camera access only to detect your body movement for gameplay (swinging your hands to swat mosquitoes). All image processing happens directly on your device using the MediaPipe Pose library.

  • Camera images and video are never recorded, stored, or sent to any server.
  • The camera is active only while the game is open.
  • You can revoke camera permission at any time in your system settings; the game will no longer be playable, but no other data is affected.

3. Advertising

The game shows rewarded and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device data (advertising ID, approximate region) under Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can decline to watch ads without affecting basic gameplay.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect children's data. Young players should have an adult help them grant camera permission.
